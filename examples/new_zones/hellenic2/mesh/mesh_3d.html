hellenic2: mesh in 3D

30 x 7 = 210 unit sources (columns x rows) of hellenic2, with the SLAB contours they were cut from. The target cell size was 50 x 50 km. Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, hover a dot to see the cell's L (along strike) and W (down dip). The plots need an internet connection (Plotly is loaded from a CDN).
