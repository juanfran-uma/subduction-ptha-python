Your own runs

Zones you can run

Every zone name that generate.py accepts for --zone, the SLAB region and SLAB product each one is built from, and the rules that tie the names together. With --ptha false (the default) the valid names are the 36 zones in lib/berryman_params.py (ZONE_TO_BERRYMAN_SEGMENTS), because a zone's seismogenic cutoff, coupling, b-value and largest observed magnitude come from Berryman et al. (2015) through that file. With --ptha true the valid names are the 51 zones that have an unsegmented row in rptha's sourcezone_parameters.csv.

At a glance

Each run needs two names. The first argument picks the SLAB raster, and --zone picks the zone's parameters.

The region

The first argument of generate.py: a SLAB region, by its three-letter prefix (ker) or part of its title (kermadec). The prefix always works. python ptha_python/list_regions.py prints the regions.

The zone

--zone: a name from the table below, spelled exactly (ryuku, not ryukyu, is the spelling PTHA18 used). It sets the Berryman et al. (2015) values and, for six zones, the trench ends.

The folder

--folder: any path relative to the repository root. It is never overwritten: a name that is taken becomes NAME_2.

Rules for the names

  • A zone name is not always a region name. The first argument is matched against the region titles and prefixes. generate.py antilles2 fails with "no SLAB2.0 region matches", because no region title is part of antilles2. A zone name that contains a region title does match (kermadectonga2 contains kermadec), but solomon2 and newhebrides do not match a region. Always give the region and --zone: generate.py caribbean --zone antilles2.
  • The trailing 2 chooses the SLAB product. A zone whose name ends in 2 is built from the SLAB2.0 raster, and any other zone from SLAB1.0. The two names are different zones: kermadectonga (SLAB1.0) and kermadectonga2 (SLAB2.0) are both in the table.
  • A name that is not in the table. With --ptha false the program accepts it, prints a note, and step 1 then stops because the zone has no Berryman et al. (2015) mapping (for example cascadia2). With --ptha true a name without a row in sourcezone_parameters.csv is refused at once.
  • Some zones are cut at the trench end points of Berryman et al. (2015), automatically and without an option. These are marked in the table.
  • Without --zone, the zone is found from the region's name. It works when exactly one zone name matches (cascadia, alaska, kuril). It stops with "matches more than one zone" when a region holds several, as kermadec (kermadectonga and kermadectonga2) and hellenic do, and it finds no zone at all for regions such as caribbean or vanuatu. Pass --zone whenever in doubt.
  • Retired zones. PTHA18 retired some zones in favour of a "2" version by giving them a weight of 0 (the table marks them). They can be generated, but step 8 stops for them (Troubleshooting). The run itself needs no PTHA18 file.

Check the resultThe program does not check that the region fits the zone. Look at the step 1 figure, and at the trench it finds, before running the rest of the steps.

Zones for --ptha true

sourcezone_parameters.csv has 93 rows. 51 of them have no segment_name, and those 51 names are what --zone accepts with --ptha true. Of the 51, 29 are in the table above (every zone of the table except the seven marked "no" in the PTHA18 column). The other 22 have no Berryman et al. (2015) mapping, so the program prints "NOT AVAILABLE" for their cutoff and step 1 stops for them:

arutrough, banda_detachment, flores, floreswetar, macquarieislandnorth, macquarienorth, moresby_trough, mussau, north_sulawesi, outer_rise_timor, outerrise_kermadectonga, outerrise_puysegur, outerrisenewhebrides, outerrisesolomon, outerrisesunda, sangihe_backthrust, se_sulawesi, seram_thrust, seramsouth, tanimbar, tolo_thrust, trobriand.

They are outer-rise, backthrust, detachment, transform and small local thrust sources, which Berryman et al. (2015) do not treat as subduction interface segments. In practice the names that can run with --ptha true are the 29 zones that appear in both lists.

Adding a zone

A zone that PTHA18 never modelled, or one that is not in the table, is added in lib/berryman_params.py. Its name goes into ZONE_TO_BERRYMAN_SEGMENTS (the Berryman et al. (2015) Table 3.1 rows it covers) and ZONE_DOMINANT_SEGMENT (the row that sets its seismogenic cutoff). Rows that Table 3.1 has but BERRYMAN_ROWS lacks must be added too. Without that mapping the zone's cutoff, coupling and b-value are not known, and step 1 stops. If the zone is one part of a SLAB region, ZONE_TRENCH_ENDS holds its two end points. Run generate.py with --ptha false; it needs no row in sourcezone_parameters.csv.

The zone list is the set of zones in lib/berryman_params.py; the SLAB product follows the trailing-2 rule of step 1. Counts are those of sourcezone_parameters.csv and lib/berryman_params.py.
